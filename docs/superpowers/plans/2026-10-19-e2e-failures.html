Root causes

  1. Add-expense form moved to signal forms. ExpensesPage.totalAmountInput is input[formControlName="amount"] (and allocatedAmount, assignedAmount for the other money fields). The template now binds [formField]="expenseForm.amount" with no formControlName attribute and no data-testid on the amount, proportional or per-split inputs. 10 failures, plus most of the 29 did-not-run tests. Fix direction: add test ids to those inputs in the template, then update the page object.
  2. Empty-state text changed. History now shows "Once payments are recorded in your group, you'll see a history of payments here..." and Summary shows "Once you have unpaid expenses in a group, you'll see a summary of who owes what here...". The page objects look for "No history found" and "No outstanding expenses found". 2 failures.
  3. Groups page for a brand-new user has no group-select. The legacy flow expects the select to exist before any group is created. 2 failures.
  4. Unawaited redirect. auth-authenticated.spec.ts:36 reads page.url() immediately after app-root appears, racing the async guard. 1 failure (passed 3 of 3 when re-run alone).
  5. Flake under parallel load. groups-admin.spec.ts:157 saw the "Your name in the group" input empty after fill() (passed 3 of 3 when re-run alone). 1 failure, which then cascades to 7 did-not-run tests.

Also for Plan 2 (tolerated until then): several legacy specs and page objects still hard-code the default dev emulator ports 8080/9099 (auth/auth.spec.ts, auth/auth-authenticated.spec.ts, smoke/homepage.spec.ts make read-only GETs; e2e/constants/index.ts defines the dev-port URLs). They passed here only because the owner's dev emulators were running. Many page objects still use waitForTimeout, and every legacy spec uses the preserveDataFirebasePage alias with its own createTestUser instead of signInAs and the seeded data.
