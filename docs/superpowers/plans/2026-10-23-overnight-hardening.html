Questions for you (most important first)

Answered by the owner on 2026-10-23: 1 keep the limits (revisit if usage grows); 2 narrow it (done, 91de5475); 3 yes, skip groups already linked (done, 3387f2e6); 4 and 5 accept for now; 6 the wording is fine. 7 is still open.

  1. Are the limits right? 100 group-mail recipients per account per day, and one confirmation email per address per hour. Each is a single constant. My recommendation: keep both for now, and look at real usage after a few weeks.
  2. The App Check error classification is out of date. isLikelyAppCheckError treats a bare Firestore permission-denied as an App Check failure, but you turned off Firestore App Check enforcement on 8/19. A rules denial therefore opens the App Check help dialog, and with this branch edit-member does too. My recommendation: narrow it to functions/unauthenticated and storage/unauthenticated while Firestore stays unenforced. It's a small follow-up.
  3. Invite linking can link one user to two rows in the same group. When linkInvitedMembers finds an unlinked placeholder in a group where you're already linked, it links you there anyway. updateMemberEmails copes by counting one row as a conflict. Should invite linking skip groups where the caller is already linked? My recommendation: yes.
  4. A retried Request All after a partial send re-requests everyone. This only happens in groups of 27 or more members when the caller is near the cap. The snackbar says how many went out. My recommendation: accept it for now.
  5. The cooldown's trade-offs. A group member can learn whether an address got a confirmation email in the last hour, and someone could keep an address in cooldown to block its confirmations. Both are documented in the follow-ups doc. My recommendation: accept them for now.
  6. Wording. The retry message is "Couldn't check your account right now. Please try again." (or "...try the link again." on the confirm page). The duplicate-address message is now the same everywhere.
  7. The Phase 3 design's own 6 questions. The top one is whether to ship in two releases, with the rules change 7 days after the code. See section 11 of the design doc.
