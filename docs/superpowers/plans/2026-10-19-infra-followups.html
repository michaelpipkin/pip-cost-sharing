  • Owner, production risk (found by 2f): the Account email change uses Firebase updateEmail. The Auth emulator accepts it, but on a project with email enumeration protection (the default for new projects) production Auth rejects it with auth/operation-not-allowed, and the app then shows only "Something went wrong - your email address could not be updated". The supported call is verifyBeforeUpdateEmail, which sends a link to the new address and changes the email only when it is followed. updateEmail also needs a recent sign-in (auth/requires-recent-login, which falls into the same generic message). Check the project's Authentication settings (Email Enumeration Protection) and try the change in production; the e2e suite cannot see this.
